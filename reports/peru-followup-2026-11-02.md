# Peru follow-up — 2026-11-02

## Decision

The public conversational catalog remains **93 access points**, including **29 UNVERIFIED** candidates, across **43 country/project associations**. No additional qualifying web-chat service or independently trained Peruvian foundation model was verified in this review. The separate Peru research/deployment watchlist grows from 12 to **14** records.

| Lead | Evidence and decision |
|---|---|
| MAIA / MYPE AsesorIA | HacerPerú runs a free WhatsApp business adviser with Peru's Ministry of Production (PRODUCE). Publisher documentation describes country-specific retrieval and LLM-generated advice; official ministry material confirms the 2026 cooperation and public WhatsApp access. **Watchlist only:** no separate public web-chat interface under current Atlas criteria; not evidence of a new Peruvian base model. |
| Quechua Collao LLM benchmark | Pocco and Oncevay, AmericasNLP 2026, present a controlled morphosyntactic dataset and evaluate existing LLMs. **Watchlist only:** research evaluation rather than a newly released model or public chat. |
| Jeimy AI | The publisher's current pages present Colibrí (limited free tier), Águila and Apu. Public presentation content is retrievable, but no successful login or conversation was independently reproduced. Architecture, base models and training provenance remain unverified. **Retain UNVERIFIED**. |
| Government and academia | PCM's Peru–Chile LatamGPT cooperation remains a regional partnership, not a distinct Peruvian model. The 2026–2030 AI strategy governs and catalogs AI activity; it does not by itself announce a released national LLM. Existing UNI/UNMSM/UPCH/UTP and other leads remain in earlier reviews. |
| Wikuña | The model card says it is based on Llama 3 8B and proposes Quechua interaction, but the visible repository tree contains documentation without public weight artifacts. Peruvian institutional ownership and a working chat are not established; **not cataloged**. |

This review used publisher and institutional pages plus the ACL Anthology. It did not send prompts through authenticated services, install apps, inspect model weights or establish access from Peru. Marketing statements are attributed to publishers rather than independently validated.
