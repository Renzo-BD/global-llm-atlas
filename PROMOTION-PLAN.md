## One-minute demonstration storyboard

0–8 s — “Which conversational AI services are accessible in different countries?”
8–20 s — Open the map, choose a country, filter statuses.
20–33 s — Demonstrate capabilities, access and UNVERIFIED labels.
33–45 s — Show Android explorer and official access links.
45–55 s — Open JSON/CSV and CONTRIBUTING.
55–60 s — “Explore, verify, contribute — and star if useful.”

## Region-focused articles (editorial, not false verification)

1. **Latin America:** how to find and verify public conversational AI projects; demonstrate country filters; explain why national origin and company domicile differ.
2. **Europe:** compare distribution of documented public chat services by country; distinguish evidence and unknown fields.
3. **Asia:** spotlight geographic variation and why access in one region does not guarantee access globally.
4. **Android:** native apps versus regional app stores versus PWAs, and why web and mobile access statuses are independent.

Every article should cite the live snapshot, state the date, link both the atlas and source repo, and never present unverified entries as confirmed.
