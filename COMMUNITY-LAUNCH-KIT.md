## English — GitHub / LinkedIn / DEV

**Headline:** Global LLM Atlas: an open, country-by-country map of conversational AI

What conversational AI services can people actually access around the world?

I’m building **Global LLM Atlas**, an open-source, versioned directory and interactive world map of public conversational LLM/MLLM services. It tracks country/project association, access requirements, capabilities, provenance, lifecycle and Android availability.

The current snapshot contains **74 access points (9 unverified candidates)** and **39 country/project associations**. Unknown data is marked as unknown rather than guessed.

🗺️ Explore: https://renzo-bd.github.io/global-llm-atlas/
💻 Dataset / source: https://github.com/Renzo-BD/global-llm-atlas

I'm looking for evidence-backed corrections and suggestions, particularly from underrepresented countries. If you find it useful, a GitHub ⭐ helps others discover it.

## Hacker News — Show HN (English)

**Title:** Show HN: Global LLM Atlas – a curated map of public conversational AI by country

I built an interactive, open-source atlas of publicly accessible conversational AI services around the world, with a JSON/CSV dataset and country, lifecycle, provenance and Android metadata.

The interesting curation problem is not whether a URL responds, but whether a public conversational experience actually works, whether free access is documented and whether the claimed country/model origin is supported by evidence. Unverified candidates are explicitly labeled.

Live demo: https://renzo-bd.github.io/global-llm-atlas/
Source: https://github.com/Renzo-BD/global-llm-atlas

Feedback on coverage and evidence standards is welcome.

## Reddit — r/SideProject or other communities **only where rules allow**

**Title:** I built an open atlas of conversational AI tools by country — feedback on curation wanted

I’ve been putting together a public world map and reusable dataset of conversational AI access points by country. Rather than list every claimed model as fact, the atlas distinguishes verified entries, limitations and unverified candidates.

There are currently 74 access points (including 9 unverified) and 39 country/project associations, with a separate view of Android availability.

Demo: https://renzo-bd.github.io/global-llm-atlas/
Repository: https://github.com/Renzo-BD/global-llm-atlas

If you use regional AI platforms that international lists overlook, I’d appreciate corrections and official links. What other evidence fields would make this more useful?

## Outreach protocol

1. Confirm the destination community allows project posts; do not bulk-post or send unsolicited mass DMs.
2. Match the post to the audience and answer comments.
3. Share the live demo first for general audiences and the repository first for developers.
4. Invite evidence-based contributions, not reciprocal stars.
5. Monitor stars, external referrals, issues and data corrections; keep metrics separate from promotional targets.
6. Recheck snapshot numbers before reusing these posts after an atlas update.
